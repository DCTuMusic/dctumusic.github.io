summary: |
    The work focus on how people live in the city nowadays. City is a form that people live in to get a more convenient life and higher salary. Everyone focuses on their works and goals to make a better life instead of building a deepe connection to their environment and ignore things that are irrelevant to their purpose. It’s the atmosphere of the city drives the people and make them unconsciousness.

    Anthrophonic sound elements from buses, metro, escalator, train and so on are processed and played in the gallery with 8 speakers to create an artificial ambience. The loudness is low enough that if the audience only focus on other works in the gallery, they will ignore the sound.
