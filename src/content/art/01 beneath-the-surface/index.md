summary: |

    Water is vibrated and transformed by sonic resonance, taking on ever-changing forms like the surface of the sea shifting through time. When we look upward, it is as if we were gazing from beneath the water, with sunlight breaking through the surface and falling softly upon us. In this work, Yen-Hao Tu uses sound waves to bring water into resonance, generating varied textures and forms. As these forms are cast onto the walls through light and shadow, the space becomes a world beneath the surface.
