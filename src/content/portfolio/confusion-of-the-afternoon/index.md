summary: |
  Concept & Narrative Alignment
  During pre-production, the director and I established Tango as the film's core musical identity. Beyond complementing an on-screen dance sequence, tango's innate push-and-pull rhythm and fatalistic undertone served as an auditory metaphor for the protagonist's psychological struggle, caught between the impulse to reach out and the instinct to withdraw.

  Orchestration & Duality
  The instrumentation intentionally bifurcates reality and desire. An intimate, pure piano voice plays the lead melody, functioning as the character's internal monologue. In contrast, lush orchestral passages are reserved exclusively for the protagonist's romanticized daydreams of intimacy, utilizing sonic scale to delineate the boundary between suppressed reality and vivid longing.

  調性確立與戲劇動機
  製作前期即與導演達成共識，確立以探戈（Tango）作為整部作品的核心聽覺語彙。此設定既呼應了劇中的舞蹈段落，更借助探戈音樂特有的停頓、推進與宿命感，將角色在渴望靠近與克制退縮之間的心理博弈，轉化為具體的節奏張力。

  配器思維與虛實對照
  在配器處理上，我運用樂器質地的反差建立主角的虛實邊界。以純淨的鋼琴聲線彈奏主旋律，作為角色的內心情感獨白；管弦樂編制則專門留給想像段落，在主角腦海中描繪與對方互動時燦爛而美好的理想化畫面，透過配器規模的轉換，精準分割現實的壓抑與想像的美滿。
