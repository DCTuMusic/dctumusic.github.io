  Narrative Arc & Experimental Electronics
  Silence draws its inspiration from the eerie quiet during the pandemic lockdowns. As the bustle of human voices vanished, the street was stripped down to traffic hums, construction noise, wind, rain, and the sounds of nature. Using this stark auditory memory as a foundation, I combined experimental synthesis with urban field recordings to establish the work’s sonic backdrop, reflecting modern urban isolation under sudden paralysis. Spanning over 40 minutes, the score traces an emotional trajectory from cold detachment to renewal: beginning with dry, mechanical signals, acoustic instruments, rich synthesizers, and layered soundscapes gradually enter to expand the space. In the final movement, warm piano melodies and environmental textures emerge, concluding the dance on an organic, hopeful note. This transition reflects society awakening from stagnation, embodying how people, despite disruption and trauma, inevitably find a path forward.

  Wearable Tracking & Live Performance
  Alongside composition, I engineered an interactive tracking system for Body EDT Dance Company. Dancers wore HTC Vive Trackers to capture live movement data, which was processed in Unity, streamed via OSC protocols, and calculated in Max/MSP to translate speed and displacement directly into musical parameters. Here, the body was no longer driven by the score, but actively generated the sound. Outside the algorithmic processing, I performed live on acoustic drums, engaging in real-time dialogue with the dancers on stage. Contemporary dance relies on breath and spontaneous presence, defying rigid tempos or pre-recorded audio. Keeping the instrumentation live allowed the music to adapt organically to each moment, imbuing every staging with unscripted vitality.

  作品敘事與實驗聲響
  《Silence》創作靈感源自三級警戒期間街頭異常的寂靜。當人聲自城市全面抽離，留存下來的僅剩車流、工地機具、風雨與蟲鳴鳥叫。我以此特殊的聽覺記憶為基底，結合實驗合成聲響與真實採集的城市環境音，構建整部作品的底色，精準呈現當代都市在突如其來的靜止中所顯露的冷調與孤立感。舞作全長約 40 餘分鐘，配樂架構了一條從封閉疏離走向生命復甦的情感軌跡：整部作品自序段冰冷、乾燥的機械訊號與無機聲響出發，隨著演出推進，聲場中陸續加入原聲樂器、飽滿的合成器與多層次音效，豐富了空間的維度；在最後段落，溫潤的鋼琴聲線與自然環境音在音樂中現身，以富有生機的暖調為舞蹈作結。這段由冷至暖的演變，既描寫了生活自三級警戒的停滯中重新甦醒，也象徵著人們即便歷經疫情的動盪與創傷，終將在日常中重新尋得前行的路徑。

  穿戴式裝置與現場演奏的現場性
  除了音樂創作，我為體相舞蹈劇場（Body EDT Dance Company）開發了一套動作互動系統。舞者配戴的 HTC Vive Tracker 捕捉即時肢體動作，數據導入 Unity 進行空間座標處理，再經由 OSC 通訊協定傳輸至 Max/MSP 運算，將動作速度與位移即時轉譯為音樂參數。在本作中，身體不再被動地受音樂驅動，而是主動成為聲響生成的源頭。在程式運算之外，我以鼓組於演出現場即興演奏，即時回應舞者在台上的身體動能。現代舞具備獨特的呼吸節奏與當下性，難以被固定拍值或預錄音檔所框限；透過將演奏置於現場，聲響得以隨著舞者的每口呼吸與現場狀態靈活流動，為演出注入不可預期的有機生命力。
