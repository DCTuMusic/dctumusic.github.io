summary: |
  Concept & Live Performance
  The Ocean is a reflective immersive theater by artist Ping-Yeh Li at U-108 SPACE, National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts (NTMoFA). The work is built around four dimensions of the ocean: the Ocean of Life, Information, Reflection, and Hope. I was invited to perform live at the opening, engaging in a real-time dialogue between music and the visuals. Using Ambisonics spatial audio on the venue's immersive multi-channel system, I moved sounds freely around the room and enveloped the audience.

  Multi-channel System & On-Site Tuning
  Beyond the opening performance, I helped set up and test the immersive sound environment during installation. Working with the venue's 16.4-channel speaker layout, I listened in the room again and again and fine-tuned how the sound was distributed, making sure it spread evenly across U-108's unusual circular space. This gave the exhibition a clear, comfortable listening experience at the official opening.
summaryZh: |
  概念與現場演出
  《海》是藝術家李炳曄於國美館 U-108 SPACE 打造的反思性沉浸劇場，作品以生命、信息、反思與希望四種維度的「海」為核心主軸。我受邀擔任展覽開幕的現場演出，透過音樂與現場影像進行即時的動態對話。結合 U-108 現地的沉浸式多聲道系統，我運用 Ambisonics 空間音訊技術，讓聲音在展場中自由遊走、環繞觀眾，為開幕帶來包覆感強烈的聽覺體驗。

  多聲道系統與現場調整
  除了開幕演出，我也在布展期間協助測試與架設現場的沉浸式聲音環境。配合場館 16.4 聲道的喇叭配置，我在現場反覆聆聽並微調聲音的空間分佈，確認聲音在 U-108 的特殊空間中能均勻擴散，讓展覽在正式展出時呈現清楚且舒適的聆聽狀態。
