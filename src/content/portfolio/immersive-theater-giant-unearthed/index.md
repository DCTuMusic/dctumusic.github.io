summary: |
  Exhibition Context & Role
  For the 2025 Whale Dig exhibition at the National Museum of Natural Science, the immersive theater served as a core centerpiece. Directed by the Li Ping-Yeh Tech-Art Lab, I was brought on board to lead the sound design and spatial audio engineering.

  Spatial Setup & Immersive Sound System
  The theater is an oval room about 14 meters wide with 4.2-meter curved walls, surrounding the audience in projection and sound. Working with the museum team, I planned an 8-channel speaker layout and mapped the spatial audio accordingly. Because an elliptical room naturally boosts or cuts certain frequencies, I spent time listening on-site during installation, testing volume levels and room reflections so I could adjust the mix directly for the space. In addition, I worked closely with the museum and video technical teams to ensure rock-solid audio-visual synchronization.

  Narrative Arc & Scientific Accuracy
  Structured around the cycle of life, the visual piece moves from the excavation and reassembly of fossil bones to rebirth, environmental pollution, ecological catastrophe, and finally the peaceful descent of a whale fall. I managed music curation, editing, audio processing, and sound design to guide this narrative progression. For a natural science museum, the sound had to balance emotional resonance with scientific integrity. All whale vocalizations strictly utilized official museum field recordings, with post-production limited to noise reduction and spatialization to preserve biological authenticity.

  展覽背景與角色
  2025 年國立自然科學博物館推出《鯨掘特展》，其中的沉浸式劇場為展覽核心展區之一，由李炳曄科技藝術實驗室統籌製作，我受邀負責整體聲音設計與沉浸式音響工程。

  空間與沈浸式系統
  劇場是一個寬約 14 公尺、高 4.2 公尺的橢圓形空間，環繞弧形牆面讓觀眾被影像與聲音包圍。我與團隊協同規劃了 8 顆喇叭組成的環繞聲場，並據此設計空間的聲音配置。由於橢圓空間容易讓特定頻率被放大或抵消，布展進場時我親自到現場聆聽，反覆測試展場的音量與回音反應，在現場直接微調混音，找出最適合該空間的聽覺平衡。同時，也與館方及影像技術團隊密切配合，完成穩定的影音同步。

  敘事脈絡與科學真實性
  展演影片以「生命循環」為核心軸線，從化石出土、骨骼拼湊、巨獸重生，走向環境汙染與生態浩劫，最終收束於鯨落的沉寂。我統籌全片的音樂選曲、剪輯、後製混音與音效設計，使聲響緊扣敘事轉折推進。身為自然科學博物館的展覽，聲音在追求感染力的同時更必須確保科學真實性。所有鯨豚鳴聲皆嚴格採用館方審定的學術錄音素材，後製僅進行降噪清理與空間定位營造，在不破壞生物聲音真實樣貌的前提下完成聽覺呈現。
