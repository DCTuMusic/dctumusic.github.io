summary: |
  Scoring & Sonic Architecture
  To sonify the narrative arc of a puppet awakening into a human being, I developed a transitional sonic palette moving from mechanical detachment to organic vitality. The score opens with irregular experimental electronics and acousmatic music, using dissonant frequencies and processed acoustic textures to establish a cold, vacant environment.

  Dramatic Progression
  As the character discovers its humanity, organic string pulses subtly infiltrate the electronic texture, acting as a surrogate for an awakening heartbeat. The composition gradually builds and resolves into an exuberant, lush ballet movement, tightly synchronizing with the choreography to deliver an emotionally resonant transformation.
summaryZh: |
  概念與聲響設計
  為了在聽覺上實踐「從偶蛻變為人」的敘事線，我將配樂分為質地截然不同的兩個階段。序段採用不規則實驗電子（Electroacoustic），以不協和頻率與經過數位處理的樂器質地，具象化偶最初所處的冰冷與虛無。

  戲劇張力推進
  隨著角色逐漸尋得人性的形廓，我讓真實弦樂的有機脈動悄然滲入底層，模擬心跳與血液流動的溫度。終段則全面釋放電子拘束，轉化為華麗奔放的古典芭蕾樂章，讓音樂與舞者的騰躍完全咬合，完成從機械冷冽到肉身溫度的完整聽覺弧線。
