summary: |
  Sonic Positioning & Seasonal Recognition
  A sound design for the Taoyuan Museum of Fine Arts' Christmas motion greeting. With only seconds to work with, the sound had to register as "Christmas" almost instantly, so I drew on the melodic vocabulary audiences already associate with the season, using just a few notes to trigger that recognition immediately.

  Timbre & Visual Correspondence
  I chose Bells to anchor the palette due to it's bright metallic overtones are among the most direct sonic signifiers of Christmas. Sound events are pinned to the motion's transition points, giving every graphic shift an audible response and locking the score to O.OO's  visual language.

  Seamless Looping
  Unlike a conventional motion graphic spot, a greeting like this plays on a continuous loop. The sound had to stay listenable across repeated plays and join end to end without a seam: I trimmed the reverb tail from the end of the file and crossfaded it into the opening, so the loop point becomes inaudible.

  聲音定位與節慶印象
  為桃園美術館的聖誕動態賀卡設計聲音。在極短的篇幅內，聲音必須在幾秒之內就讓人認出「聖誕節」，因此我從大眾耳熟能詳的聖誕旋律取材，以幾個關鍵的音符建構對節慶的聯想。

  音色選擇與視覺呼應
  音色上以鐘琴（Bell）為主體，清亮的金屬泛音本身即是聖誕節最直接的聽覺符號。聲音節點緊貼動態圖形的變化落點，讓每一次轉場都有對應的聲響回饋，與 O.OO 的視覺語言相互扣合。

  無縫循環的技術處理
  與一般 motion graphic 廣告不同，這類賀卡是不斷循環播放的。聲音除了要耐聽、重複多次都不膩，頭尾還必須能自然接上。具體作法是將音檔尾端的殘響切斷，在開頭處以交叉淡入（crossfade）銜接，讓循環的接點聽不出斷點。
