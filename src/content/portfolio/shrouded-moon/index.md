  Eastern Aesthetics & Acoustic Transformation
  Echoing the choreographer’s Eastern movement vocabulary, the score opens gently with soft, sustained strings. By processing acoustic strings and woodwinds through electronic sound design, the textures unfold in delicate layers, evoking the visual sensation of parting clouds gradually revealing a full moon.

  Dramatic Friction & Resolution
  The middle section introduces unfamiliar, experimental electronic textures, carving out the physical tension and confrontations between the dancers. As these bodily conflicts soften toward resolution, the score pivots back into harmonic balance, building up ascending layers of acoustic strings that guide the performance to a warm, resonant finale.
summaryZh: |
  東方意象與聲響調性
  呼應編舞家具東方韻味的肢體語彙，配樂以柔和的長音弦樂緩慢開場。我將原聲弦樂與管樂導入電子效果器進行即時調變與層疊，模擬滿月之際雲層逐層散開、月色漸顯的空靈意境，為舞台鋪墊內斂而具呼吸感的底色。

  戲劇對立與結構推進
  中段轉入冷冽且異質的實驗電子聲響，運用碎裂微粒與失真頻率，雕刻舞者肢體間的激烈碰撞與心理對立。隨著劇中衝突逐步化解、回歸平衡，樂音漸次褪去尖銳質地，由溫暖的弦樂聲部再度疊加、爬升，引導整體動態平穩收束於和諧而寬廣的終段。
