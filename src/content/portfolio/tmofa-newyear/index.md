summary: |
  Cultural Symbols & Timbral Selection
  Designed the sound for the Taoyuan Museum of Fine Arts (TMoFA) New Year motion poster. I anchored the sonic palette with three iconic traditional instruments: Chinese drum, cymbals, and gong, using their crisp, resonant timbres to immediately establish a vibrant festive atmosphere.

  Everyday Soundscapes & Symbolic Juxtaposition
  Alongside acoustic instruments, authentic firecracker sound effects were integrated into the soundscape. Festive memory thrives not only on musical conventions but also on the auditory textures of daily life during the season. Juxtaposing symbolic instrumentation with real-world sonic memories reinforces a shared cultural nostalgia.

  Seamless Loop Engineering
  Designed for continuous playback across digital platforms, the audio needed to remain engaging across repeats while maintaining an imperceptible transition point. To achieve a seamless loop, the tail reverb was precisely cut and reintroduced at the head using subtle crossfading, effectively eliminating audible splices and ensuring an uninterrupted auditory cycle.

  節慶符號與音色轉譯
  為桃園市立美術館的新年動態賀卡規劃聲音設計。我選取傳統大鼓、銅鈸與鑼三種最具象徵性的打擊樂器，提煉其明亮而具穿透力的音色特質，迅速建立年節熱鬧且莊重的聽覺基調。

  生活音景與符號並置
  除了器樂演奏，聲場中亦融入真實的鞭炮音效。新年的文化記憶不僅來自節奏明快的音樂，更深植於生活街道中的環境聲響；透過將傳統器樂符號與真實音景疊合並置，喚起受眾對春節現場感的直覺共鳴。

  無縫循環的工程處理
  因應動態賀卡在社群與展演空間的重複播放需求，聲音架構必須兼顧耐聽度與流暢性。為實現完美的無縫循環（Seamless Loop），我精確截取尾段殘響，並於開端巧妙運用交叉淡化（Crossfade）進行重疊銜接，消除任何切斷感與相位抵消，使接點完全隱形於播放流程中。
