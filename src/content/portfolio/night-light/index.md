summary: "For this film, I built the score by layering synthesizers arranged through an orchestral, multi-part harmonic approach. The slowly shifting synthetic timbres create an unfamiliar, detached texture that supports the film's quiet pacing. In the closing sequence, as the boy gazes at the factory chimney and imagines the city lights of Taipei, I wove metallic industrial foley sounds into the musical arrangement, blending instruments with sound design into a singular auditory experience."
summaryZh: "這部作品我以合成器作為主要聲響核心進行堆疊。聲部編排借鑒管弦樂的和弦思維，但運用合成器讓聲音緩慢流動、推移，製造出一種疏離且陌生的空間質地，為影片建立抽離的調性。在片尾男孩看著廠房煙囪、投射對台北光景想像的段落，我進一步將音效設計生成的工廠敲擊聲融入音樂之中，將原聲、樂器與環境音效化為一體，完成一段獨特的聽覺體驗。"
