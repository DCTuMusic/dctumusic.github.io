summary: |
  Folds of Memory is a 3D motion project that explores space and nostalgic atmosphere, drawing inspiration from Taiwanese department stores. Following a paper crane through stylized environments, the work focuses on building a quiet world filled with memory and atmosphere, as the crane interacts with different objects and scenes throughout its journey.

  Composed for piano and orchestra, the score adds a warm, lively texture to the animation. Its musical density follows the story, remaining light when entering the store, and swelling into a majestic orchestral climax as the paper crane soars through the space.
  
summaryZh: |
